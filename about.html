About Daily Bread

A daily biblical message, prayer reminder, and counselling app for believers.

Our Mission

Daily Bread exists to share God's word daily, encourage believers through prayer, and provide a private space for counselling and spiritual guidance.

Daily Messages

Read a fresh biblical message every day in English or Malayalam. React with a like or dislike to let us know the message touched you.

Prayer Reminders

Enable push notifications to receive a daily prayer reminder at the scheduled time. Take a moment to pause, pray, and be still.

Counselling

Need someone to talk to? Submit a private counselling request — your contact details and message are kept strictly confidential.
